# v3.8.1: Read the plan summary on the approval card

Open a plan approval card to see what will change, the main risk, and how the work will be verified. The board now reads the standard plan template’s `## Summary` when `## At a glance` is missing or empty. Existing plans work without edits.

A populated `## At a glance` still takes precedence. If the document has no summary, the card points to the full text under More.
